2 Problem and users

Observed in university lectures: professors speak at 135–160 WPM across EN, RU, and KZ, while students write only 25–35 WPM and hoard 50-min voice memos or messy PDF slides nobody re-reads. Goal: record live speech or upload files for instant structured learning.

🎧
Target Users — University Students (Aged 17–22):
1st–4th year BSc students. Goal: record live mic or upload audio/PDFs → get clear Kazakh / RU / EN notes & quizzes in noisy (>60 dB) halls.

7 Main functions (step-by-step on live website)

1. Record Speech or Upload Files: live mic with dB sensor (42 dB, SNR +18.6 dB) or direct audio/PDF upload with Biquad filter.
2. Kazakh / RU / EN Notes: structured lecture summary, Key Points, Glossary & PDF export.
3. Self-Check Quiz & AI Tutor: 8 multiple-choice questions in Kazakh/RU/EN + 24/7 AI Teacher.
4. Cloud History & Scores: all recorded/uploaded lectures, Kazakh/RU/EN notes & quiz results saved in PostgreSQL.
