1. Can you listen, comprehend, and write complete lecture notes at the same time?

38 responses · Single choice

  • No — I fall behind and miss the explanation 76.3% (29)
  • Yes — I manage, but with high stress 23.7% (9)

2. How often do you re-listen to raw 50-minute voice memos recorded during lectures?

38 responses · Single choice

  • Never — scrubbing an hour of audio takes too long 81.6% (31)
  • Rarely — only the night before a final exam 13.2% (5)
  • Frequently 5.2% (2)

3. Do you struggle with STEM terminology when professors mix English, Russian, and Kazakh?

38 responses · Single choice

  • Yes — especially translating complex algorithms into Kazakh 68.4% (26)
  • Sometimes 23.7% (9)
  • No — fluent in all three 7.9% (3)

38 responses · Single choice
